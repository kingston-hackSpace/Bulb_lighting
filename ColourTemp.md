# Colour Temperature

Colour temperature is measured in Kelvin (K) and describes how warm or cool the light appears. Lower values (2700–3000K) produce a warm, yellowish light suitable for a cosy atmosphere. Higher values (4000–5000K) produce a cool, white daylight light, better suited for workspaces and studios where colour accuracy matters. For an art or design context, 4000–5000K is generally recommended.
