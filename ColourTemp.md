# Colour Temperature

Lighting colour temperature is measured in **Kelvin (K)** and describes how warm or cool the light appears. 

**WARM WHITE:** Lower values **(2700–3000K)** produce a warm, yellowish light suitable for a cosy atmosphere. 

**COOL WHITE:** Higher values **(4000–5000K)** produce a cool, white daylight light, better suited for workspaces and studios where colour accuracy matters.
