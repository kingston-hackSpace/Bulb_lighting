# Colour Temperature

Colour temperature is measured in **Kelvin (K)** and describes how warm or cool the light appears. 

WARM LIGHT: Lower values (2700–3000K) produce a warm, yellowish light suitable for a cosy atmosphere. 

COOL LIGHT: Higher values (4000–5000K) produce a cool, white daylight light, better suited for workspaces and studios where colour accuracy matters.
